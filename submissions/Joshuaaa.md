## WHAT IS BLOCKCHAIN?

Blockchain is a digital system for recording and storing information in blocks that are linked together using cryptographic hashes.

## WHAT PROBLEM DOES BLOCK CHAIN SOLVE?

Blockchain helps solve the problem of trust and record manipulation between people or organizations that may not fully trust each other.

It provides:

- **Security** – protects records from unauthorized changes.
- **Transparency** – participants can verify transactions.
- **Immutability** – recorded information is difficult to change.

## WHICH INDUSTRIES CAN BLOCKCHAIN BE USED IN?

| Industry | Brief Example |
|---|---|
| Banking | Faster and traceable money transfers |
| Healthcare | Secure sharing of medical records |

## WHAT IS ENCRYPTION?

Encryption is the process of converting readable information (**plaintext**) into an unreadable form (**ciphertext**) to prevent unauthorized people from understanding it.

## WHAT IS THE FUNCTION OF ENCRYPTION?

The main function of encryption is to protect information from unauthorized access.

It provides:

- **Confidentiality** – keeps information private.
- **Security** – protects sensitive data.
- **Privacy** – prevents unauthorized people from reading information.

## WHAT IS ON-CHAIN?

On-chain means that information or a transaction is recorded directly on a blockchain.

## WHAT IS A MERKLE TREE?

A Merkle Tree is a tree-like structure that uses hashes to organize and verify transactions or other data efficiently.

## WHAT IS A HASH FUNCTION?

A hash function is a mathematical function that takes data of any size and produces a fixed-length string of characters called a hash.
